IRON RING @VERSION@ for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel)
========================================================

Open @START@. macOS 11 Big Sur or later. Move it to Applications if you like.

If macOS says the app can't be checked for malicious software, open it once,
then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and choose "Open Anyway"
(on macOS 14 and earlier: Control-click the app and choose Open).

Your options, replays and F12 screenshots are kept in
~/Library/Application Support/dkapusta/Iron Ring/
