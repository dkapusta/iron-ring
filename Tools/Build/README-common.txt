
The game starts fullscreen at your desktop's resolution.

CONTROLS (default; change them in Options > Controls)

                      Player 1        Player 2           Gamepad
  Move / jump / crouch W A S D        Arrow keys         D-pad or left stick
  Light punch         F               Keypad 1           X (west)
  Heavy punch         G               Keypad 2           Y (north)
  Light kick          H               Keypad 3           A (south)
  Heavy kick          J               Keypad 0           B (east)
  Guard               Space           Right Shift        Right shoulder
                      (or hold back)  (or hold back)     (or hold back)
  Pause               Escape          Escape             Start

In menus Return confirms and Escape goes back. On the character select,
heavy punch shows the fighter's move list.

PLAYING ONLINE

Online play needs an Iron Ring server: one a friend runs, or your own.
The server is free and open source, and runs on Linux, Windows, macOS or
in Docker (docs/server.md in the source package). In ONLINE, type the
server's address into SERVER, create a room and send your friend the
invite it shows (server:port/CODE); they paste it into INVITE.
Both players need the same version of the game.
