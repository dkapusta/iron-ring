# Audio

All of Iron Ring's sound is generated by `Tools/Audio/generate_audio.py` from a model of the NES (Ricoh 2A03) sound chip: two pulse channels, the triangle and the noise channel, sequenced at 60 Hz like an NES sound driver, plus the DMC sample channel for the announcer. Decision records: [ADR-0013](adr/0013-nes-style-generated-audio.md), [ADR-0014](adr/0014-venue-themes-and-announcer.md).

```sh
pip install -r Tools/Audio/requirements.txt               # numpy, scipy (pinned with the art tools), eSpeak NG
python3 Tools/Audio/generate_audio.py                     # writes Assets/Resources/Audio/
python3 Tools/Audio/generate_audio.py --preview build/a   # also the themes as WAV, for listening
python3 Tools/Audio/generate_audio.py --check             # CI: committed files are current
```

Encoding the music needs `ffmpeg` with libvorbis; `--check` does not.

## What plays when

| Id | Played on | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `light_hit` | light attack connects | short noise burst over a falling blip |
| `heavy_hit` | heavy attack connects | deep noise hit, triangle thump, falling growl |
| `guard` | attack blocked | bright metallic 12.5 % blip |
| `breakaway` | Breakaway shove | rising double sweep over a noise whoosh |
| `tech` | throw broken | two quick high clanks |
| `slam` | throw lands | heavy floor impact with a small bounce |
| `round_bell` | "FIGHT!", time over, a drawn match | boxing bell, struck twice |
| `ko` | a round ends by K.O. | impact, then a long falling tone over a rumble |
| `victory` | the match is won | 3-second jingle, the theme's D minor resolving to D major |
| `counter` | a counter hit, over the hit sound | bright two-step clang, rising |
| `combo` | each combo hit after the first, over the hit sound | short blip, played 12 % higher per hit (up to 1.7×) |
| `menu_move` | a menu cursor or setting moves | very short, quiet high blip |
| `menu_confirm` | a menu choice, a fighter picked, the pause menu opens | two-note rising blip |
| `menu_back` | backing out, or a locked fighter | the confirm's notes, falling |

### Music: one theme per venue, and the front end's

The title, character select, stage select and options play **"Invitation Only"** (`theme_menu.ogg`, A minor, 112 BPM: slower and sparser than the venue themes, so it sits under the menu sounds; its A section quotes the main theme's opening motif). `GameSettings.json → ui.music` names it, and it keeps playing from screen to screen rather than restarting.

Each stage names its theme in `Assets/Data/stages.json → music`. The theme starts with every match (and when F2 changes the stage) and fades out when the match is decided. Every theme has an intro that plays once; the loop then returns to section A.

| Stage | Theme | Key, tempo | Character |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pier 9 | **"Iron Ring"** (`theme_pier_nine.ogg`) | D minor, 150 BPM | Driving: intro hook, A/B/A′, breakdown. Lead on pulse 1, arpeggiated chords or an echo on pulse 2, octave bass, straight drums. |
| Lantern Row | **"Curfew Market"** (`theme_lantern_row.ogg`) | A harmonic minor, ~129 BPM shuffle | Sneaky: lantern-chime intro, off-beat chord stabs, shuffled root-fifth bass, ticks on the off-beats. |
| The Foundry | **"Slag and Sparks"** (`theme_foundry.ogg`) | G minor, ~129 BPM | Mechanical: a drop-hammer ostinato on pulse 2, a lead rising in fourths, octave-pounding triangle, the metallic tick as an anvil on the off-beats. |
| The Crown | **"Crown at Dawn"** (`theme_the_crown.ogg`) | E minor, 180 BPM | The final bout: power-chord hits, galloping bass, shimmering arpeggios, the lead doubled a fifth below, a climbing finale. |

`music.json` lists the themes by id with their loop point, level and a hash of the rendered PCM.

### The announcer

The ring announcer speaks through the NES's DMC channel, the way NES games did:
- **Source:** each line is synthesised with eSpeak NG (the "announcer" voice variant).
- **Encoding:** the speech is resampled to the DMC's 33.1 kHz rate and delta-modulated to 1 bit per sample, with the 7-bit level stepping by ±2.
- **Playback:** it plays back through the 2A03 DAC curve and the same output filters as the music.

The grit and the hiss are the DMC's, not a filter.

| Id (`Voice/<id>.wav`) | When |
| --- | --- |
| `round_1` … `round_4`, `final_round` | the round is called (`final_round` when either fighter can win it, or in round 5) |
| `fight` | "FIGHT!" |
| `ko`, `double_ko`, `time_over` | the round ends (the K.O. call lands 0.4 s after the impact) |
| `<fighter>_wins`, `draw` | the match is decided (the victory jingle follows the call) |

Lines are in `ProjectSettings/GameSettings.json → voice`; AssetPacker requires the fixed lines and warns if a fighter has no `_wins` line. The announcer replaced the round-call fanfare: the line needs the whole second before "FIGHT!".

## Formats and levels

- Effects and voice: WAV, 44.1 kHz, mono, 16-bit, decoded once at load. Music: Ogg Vorbis q5, streamed.
- All files render at the same gain (`GAIN` in the generator), so their levels stay relative; the generator refuses to clip. Voice lines are normalised to −3 dBFS. Music plays at `gain` 0.5 from `music.json`.
- Mix groups: `boot.config` → `music-volume`, `sfx-volume`, `voice-volume` (0–1), or `-music-volume` / `-sfx-volume` / `-voice-volume` on the command line. Effects use a pool of eight tracks; when all are busy the oldest is cut. The announcer has one track: a new line cuts the previous one.
- The final mix passes a soft limiter (knee at −2 dBFS), so stacked impacts over the music never clip.

## Changing sounds

- An effect is a function in `Tools/Audio/chiptune/sfx.py` that writes per-frame register values (period, duty, 4-bit volume) for the channels it uses; add it to `EFFECTS`. New ids the game plays also go in `ProjectSettings/GameSettings.json → sounds` and `content::kSounds`, which AssetPacker checks.
- A theme is `Tools/Audio/chiptune/themes/<stage>.py` (registered in `THEMES` in the generator): tracker-style lines such as `"d5:4 a4:2 r:2"` (note:rows; each theme sets its frames per row) and per-frame instruments from `chiptune/instruments.py` (volume envelope, duty sequence, arpeggio, vibrato). `bars()` rejects a bar that isn't 16 rows long. A new venue's theme also goes in its `stages.json` entry.
- An announcer line is an entry in `LINES` in `Tools/Audio/chiptune/voice.py` (text, rate, pitch, range) plus `GameSettings.json → voice`; a new fighter needs `<id>_wins`.
- `chiptune/apu.py` is the chip model: period registers as on hardware, the 2A03 non-linear mixer, 4× oversampling, and Famicom-style output filters.
