# Provenance — V3B venues and V3A.1 corrections

## Inputs and recovery

The inputs were the supplied Iron Ring V3 and V3A.1 briefs, `iron-ring-main(4).zip`, the delivered V3A native character masters, and the previously saved `IronRing_ArtPack_v3.zip`. The venue layers and their authoring sources were recovered from that earlier archive. The 15 finished native venue layers are unchanged from those recovered masters; their three 640 × 360 previews were recomposed with the supplied project compositor.

The earlier archive’s provenance records OpenAI’s built-in `image_gen` tool, followed by native pixel authoring with Python, Pillow, NumPy and SciPy. It records the project’s own briefs, setting, earlier art, pose guides and generated identity studies as inputs. It states that no franchise screenshots, franchise characters or logos, real brands, real people or external reference images were used. The venues, spectators and prize-fight prints are fictional Iron Ring artwork.

## New correction work

This pass used OpenAI’s built-in `image_gen` tool for corrective studies of the Broker’s skin, Sable’s two contact poses, Idris’s two active kicks and Sable’s Wire Kick. Its model/version identifier was not exposed, so none is asserted. Inputs were the existing Iron Ring frames, palette and correction instructions only. No external visual references or real people were introduced.

Native finishing used JavaScript and Sharp: masked skin-ramp replacement, protected material pixels, palette-matched leg patches, articulated leg registration and local shoe finishing. The Broker’s final frames use a controlled material recolour. Sable’s contact legs incorporate the generated repair studies. The kicks retain their original pixel texture while the striking limbs are registered to the required boxes. The finished assets are therefore a combination of generated studies and constrained native corrections, not untouched image-model outputs.

Python 3.12.14, Pillow 12.3.0 and NumPy 2.3.5 were used for analysis, comparison previews and the supplied importer, compositor, frame reviewer and art self-test. Review images are compositions of the actual native assets. No code or game-data changes are part of the delivery.
